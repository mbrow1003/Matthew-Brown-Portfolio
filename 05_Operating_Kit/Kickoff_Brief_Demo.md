# GC Kickoff Brief — Demo Mixed-Use Tower

*A one-page brief read on a phone in the parking lot. Fictional project; the structure is the point.*

**When / where.** Tuesday, 8:00 AM, GC site office. Walk the site before or after. 45–60 minutes.

**Who's in the room.** The GC runs it. Your contracting party's PM and their superintendent sit on your side of the table. The super is your daily relationship once hardware lands — if you haven't met, that's where the small talk goes.

## How it runs

The GC works from a packet: safety and badging, owner-controlled insurance enrollment, submittal and schedule expectations, RFI and communication protocol, pay app cutoffs, lien waivers. Mostly them talking. Your asks fit under schedule and submittals. The questions that come back to you are practical: point of contact, lead time, crew size.

## Hold the line

A kickoff pulls for agreement. These three don't get settled here.

- **Commercial terms run through your contracting party.** Quote, insurance deduct, contract value — none of it is a GC conversation.
- **Temporary doors.** 60 openings across two packages. Listen to the sequencing, take notes, don't commit.
- **Insurance enrollment.** Take the packet. No deduct number in the room.

## Same day, after

Send a short recap email. A kickoff can function as de facto acceptance of whatever was said in it, and you want the record in writing if it does.

## Ask for

Line these up with your contracting party's PM beforehand, so they carry them or clear you to.

- **The plan set** — floor plans as individual sheets, door schedule, elevations, hardware spec section, ASIs and bulletins to date
- **Current revision level** — to check against what the quote was priced from
- **Platform** — does the GC mandate one, or can you run your own Fieldwire with visibility granted to them?
- **The security / access-control sub**, and the coordination path. Reader, power, and low voltage are by others on every electrified opening — the largest scope boundary on the job.
- **Floor-by-floor release sequence and dried-in dates.** Unit doors are the volume, and they come late and fast.
- **When the keying meeting happens.** Every cylinder is construction-keyed until then. Permanent keying is long-lead.
- **Storage and laydown** — plus hoisting, badging, and downtown hours. Hardware is theft-attractive.
