# SOP - Ops Cadence

Title: SOP: Ops Cadence (Weekly Update & Monthly Board)

Author: Matthew Brown

Status: Draft

Applies To: Door & Hardware Installation Projects

Version: v1.0

Last Updated: 09/26/2026

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the ops cadence. Every active job is reviewed on a fixed day, in a fixed format, so blockers and decisions surface before they turn into field problems.

The cadence works because people know what to expect, not because a policy tells them to.

The goal is to:

- put every active job in front of the ops lead and the owner on a set day

- turn open questions into yes/no decisions with a named decider

- keep status on one card per job instead of in text threads

- send crews a clear list of what to do next

## 2. Scope

Applies to every active project, from precon through closeout.

- **Weekly:** every card is updated on Monday.

- **Monthly:** the board meeting is held on the first Monday of the month.

**Roles**

| **Role** | **Part in the cadence**                                             |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ops Lead | Runs the cadence: updates cards, frames decisions, sends crew lists |
| Owner    | Decides on manpower and spend                                       |
| Foreman  | Reports field status and receives the door-number lists             |

## 3. Core Principles

1.  **Same day and same format, every week. Predictability is what makes it work.**

2.  **Each project has one card, and the card is the status.**

3.  **Decisions come first, and each is asked as yes/no.**

4.  **Nothing goes on a card as a blocker until it survives the paperwork check.**

5.  **Cards record what happened, not who caused it.**

6.  **Each message is matched to its audience.**

## 4. Definitions / Standards

### 4.1 Rhythm

| **When**                  | **What**                                                                   | **Who**         |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Every Monday              | Update every active card, flag blockers and decisions, and send crew lists | Ops Lead        |
| First Monday of the month | Board meeting: walk every card and get decisions made                      | Ops Lead, Owner |

### 4.2 Card Standard

Each project gets one card on the ops board (Trello), with five custom fields and an activity block.

| **Field**        | **Content**                                         | **Example**                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Phase            | Precon, Mobilized or Closeout                       | Mobilized                                |
| Milestone Status | The next milestone and where it stands, in one line | Level 2 import done, 180 of 190 pinned   |
| Blocker          | The one thing stopping progress, or blank           | Spec pages for two headings not received |
| Decision Needed  | A yes/no question with a named decider, or blank    | Owner: approve expedited freight?        |
| Owner            | One accountable person                              | Ops Lead                                 |

The activity block holds dated one-line entries, newest first. It records what happened.

> 04/14 Receiving reconciliation done. Short-ship: 2 closers, vendor claim filed.
>
> 04/09 Kickoff held with GC.

### 4.3 Audience Standard

| **Audience**              | **Format**                                                                                                                           |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Owner                     | A short money summary ending in a yes/no question. Have the math ready but don't open with it. Sensitive items are raised in person. |
| Foreman / crew            | Door numbers and direct instructions, sent on WhatsApp                                                                               |
| GC and executive contacts | The conclusion first. The arithmetic only if they challenge it.                                                                      |
| Suppliers                 | A net reconciliation of what was shipped and received, not who was at fault                                                          |

## 5. Process / Workflow

5.1 Weekly (Every Monday)

1.  Before Monday, refresh every card from Fieldwire, the packing slips and the latest field walk.

2.  Update all five fields and add activity lines.

3.  Before any new shortage or field claim becomes a Blocker, test it against the packing slip, the BOL and a physical count.

4.  Move anything that needs the owner into Decision Needed, phrased as a yes/no question.

5.  Send each crew its door-number list (SOP: Crew List).

5.2 Monthly Board Meeting (First Monday)

1.  **Decisions Needed first.** Ask each one as a yes/no question. Have the math ready, but don't lead with it.

2.  **Blockers next.** Give each blocker an owner and a date it will clear by.

3.  **Phase changes:** jobs moving from precon to mobilized, or from mobilized to closeout.

4.  **Precon pipeline** and new work coming in.

5.  **After the meeting,** log each decision in its card's activity block the same day.

## 6. Validation / Quality Control

- Every active project has a card.

- Every card was updated this Monday. Blocker and Decision Needed can be blank, but never out of date.

- Every Decision Needed names a decider and is phrased as a yes/no question.

- Every Blocker passed the paperwork check.

- Every decision from the board meeting was logged the same day.

- Activity entries are dated, newest first, and factual.

## 7. Boundaries / Exclusions

- The owner decides manpower and spend. The cadence brings decisions forward but doesn't make them.

- Conversations about money or sensitive issues happen in person, not on a card or by text.

- Cards are not a place for complaints about people.

- Commitments on scope, materials and change orders go through the party holding the contract, not straight to the GC.

- GC-directed changes are documented in writing and dated. Life-safety items are tracked separately from closeout items.

- The board tracks status and decisions. Task-level detail stays in Fieldwire, and quantities and billing stay in the trackers.

- The cadence doesn't enforce anything. It makes expectations predictable.

## 8. Reporting / Outputs

| **Output**                                                                                           | **When**                      | **To**                |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Updated cards                                                                                        | Every Monday                  | Ops board             |
| Crew door-number lists                                                                               | Every Monday                  | Foremen, via WhatsApp |
| Weekly progress: commercial openings complete vs. total, and unit-door level sets complete vs. total | Every Monday                  | Owner                 |
| Decision log, kept in each card's activity block                                                     | Same day as the board meeting | Ops board             |

## 9. Summary Statement

Every Monday, each active job's card is updated across its five fields, and crews get their door-number lists.

On the first Monday of the month, the ops lead walks the board with the owner. Decisions are made as yes/no and logged the same day. Keeping the timing and format predictable is what makes the cadence work.
