# Demo — Hotel Lobby Millwork, Level 1

This is a fictional project built the way a real one would be: fourteen units across five types, each one a pinned task with a gate checklist for its type. Nothing here is from a real job.

**Look at three things, in this order.**

**RD-01, the reception desk.** It's in HOLD. Field width came back 142-1/4" against 144" drawn. The compare line caught it and the release line is unchecked — the shop hasn't cut anything. That's the whole point of the gate.

**V-101, a guest room vanity.** Every line checked, accepted with a name and a date. That's what done looks like.

**WP-04, a wall panel in the shop.** Field verified, released on rev 2, being fabricated. Mid-stream.

The one rule that makes it a gate rather than a list: the two GATE lines — released and accepted — are signed by someone other than the person whose work they verify. The guy who measured doesn't release. The installer doesn't accept.
